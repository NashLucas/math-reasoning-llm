## 1. Executive Summary & Objective

In Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO), policy gradient updates are driven by group-relative advantages:
$$A_i = \frac{r_i - \mu_g}{\sigma_g + \epsilon}$$
If a prompt is either trivially easy (all completions correct, $r_i=r_{\max}$) or impossibly difficult (all completions failed, $r_i=r_{\min}$), the group standard deviation collapses ($\sigma_g = 0$) resulting in zero advantage ($A_i = 0$) and zero policy update. This pipeline filters the training corpus to select prompts within the intermediate difficulty band, maximizing advantage variance and policy gradient signal during candidate generation ($G=4$).
